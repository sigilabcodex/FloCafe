# Product discovery questions

<!-- docs:policy-allow: handoff -->

These questions require decisions from Kalali operators or owners. Product requirements do not
settle them, and no schema or technical architecture should be chosen before the business rule is
clear. Link a decision record when a question is resolved.

| ID | Open question | Why the answer matters | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KQ-01 | What is the production day for overnight work, and at what local time does its date roll over? | Demand grouping, due dates, reports and handoff depend on the same boundary. | Walk through a night bake and morning fulfillment with actual times. |
| KQ-02 | What minimum WIP detail is useful: batch, stage, quantity, expected readiness, or some combination? | Determines whether existing process steps suffice or operators need separately recorded WIP. | Observe interrupted dough/prep work and ask what the next shift must know. |
| KQ-03 | When is work an intermediate stock supply versus WIP attached to an active batch? | Prevents double-counting available stock and unfinished work. | Trace one intermediate from preparation through storage and later consumption. |
| KQ-04 | Which orders recur, how far ahead should occurrences be created, and how are exceptions and series edits handled? | Controls duplicate generation and what appears in the demand plan. | Collect real repeating customer orders and their exception examples. |
| KQ-05 | Which internal tasks recur, who owns them, and how are skipped, deferred or blocked tasks handled? | Internal production work is different from customer demand and may have its own audit needs. | List recurring routines by station/shift and current paper or verbal process. |
| KQ-06 | What is the minimum reliable shift handoff: notes, structured checklist, acknowledgment, or a hybrid? | Affects handoff speed, accountability and whether work is lost between shifts. | Rehearse outgoing/incoming shift with active batch, blocker and ready output. |
| KQ-07 | Which stock is available to promise or consume, and which is committed, quarantined, reserved or otherwise unavailable? | Existing stock balance does not automatically mean stock is available for a new order. | Review current reservation, spoilage and fulfillment practices. |
| KQ-08 | Which supplier and purchasing capabilities are in scope: directory, lists, quotes, confirmed purchases, physical receiving, invoices, or reconciliation? | The requested capability extends beyond SKOSS's explicitly deferred full-procurement scope; avoid building accounting or invoice behavior by implication. | Review current purchasing records, role ownership, and who reconciles ordered versus received inputs. |
| KQ-09 | Which price evidence is retained, and how does it relate to current WAC and recipe/batch costing? | A quote or observed shelf price must not silently become inventory cost; cost reports need a stable source rule. | Compare sample quotes, receipts, invoices, discounts, freight and existing WAC behavior. |
| KQ-10 | Which work surfaces are needed at counter, bar, prep, oven, packing and shift lead? | FloCafe already has POS, KDS and Server App; a new client should not duplicate them without evidence. | Map device, network, printer and actions per station. |
| KQ-11 | Which roles can create, start, pause, complete, cancel, adjust yield, discard, receive stock and accept a handoff? | Permission boundaries need operational approval and must remain backend-enforced. | Review current staff roles with owners and each action's risk. |
| KQ-12 | Which printed artifacts are essential at opening: receipt, KOT, batch ticket, label, packing slip, handoff sheet? | Determines whether existing document kinds cover the workflow. | Test representative thermal widths and labels on the actual devices. |
| KQ-13 | What constitutes “opening ready,” who signs off, and what date/conditions govern the rehearsal? | The roadmap cannot define release readiness without an owner and acceptance evidence. | Name accountable owners and confirm a rehearsal scenario. |
| KQ-14 | What exactly generates an inventory-needs shopping suggestion: low-stock threshold, expected production demand, lead time, safety stock, confirmed purchases, or a combination? | A suggested amount is misleading if it ignores commitments, planned demand or replenishment timing. | Walk through recent purchases and compare the proposed need with on-hand stock, pending receipts, production plans and actual use. |
| KQ-15 | What events and evidence distinguish a quotation, observed price, confirmed purchase and physical receipt? | Only physical receipt should update stock and current WAC; state changes need clear actors and evidence. | Review a quote that was not purchased, a confirmed order not yet delivered, and a partial delivery. |
| KQ-16 | Which supplier, brand and presentation identifiers are canonical, and how should aliases or duplicate catalog entries map? | Price comparisons and later imports depend on stable identities rather than text similarity. | Obtain the Kalali Catalog Maestro V4.3 and map its identifiers and fields. That artifact was not found in this repository checkout. |
| KQ-17 | How are package size, purchase unit and supply base unit converted, and who approves ambiguous or changing conversions? | Incorrect conversion makes normalized prices and received stock wrong while hiding package differences. | Collect package examples (case, bag, bottle, weight/volume) and test exact conversion rules with inventory owners. |
| KQ-18 | Can a price observation be edited, or must a correction append a superseding record? What is the retention period? | Historical trend analysis must explain who changed evidence, when and why. | Review correction, deletion, privacy and retention expectations with owners. |
| KQ-19 | How are partial receipts, substitutions, backorders, damaged quantities, discounts, taxes, freight and currency handled? | Purchase totals, physical movements and WAC can diverge if these cases have no agreed allocation rules. | Walk through real supplier documents and a delivery that differs from the confirmed quantity. |
| KQ-20 | Which devices may retain unsynchronized observations, how are local records protected, and what happens when a device is shared, lost or replaced? | Offline records can contain commercial data and may outlive a staff session or device assignment. | Identify device ownership, screen-lock/encryption capabilities, sign-out behavior and recovery expectations. |
| KQ-21 | Which identities and permissions can capture, sync, confirm a purchase, receive stock, correct a price and resolve conflicts? | These actions have different financial and stock effects and need backend-enforced attribution. | Review roles and separation of duties with purchasers and inventory owners. |
| KQ-22 | Which remote synchronization paths are authorized, and how are retries, stale credentials and conflicting edits resolved? | LAN, VPN/Tailscale and remote access have different reachability and trust boundaries; unsafe retries can duplicate evidence or receipts. | Approve supported network paths, authentication lifetime, retry behavior and operator-facing conflict cases. |

Until resolved, keep implementation reversible and do not encode assumptions into migrations.
